## Before going live — replace the placeholders

Search and replace in every `.html` file:

- `60123456789` → your WhatsApp number (international format, no `+`, spaces or leading zero)
- `+60 12-345 6789` → your number as you want it displayed
- `facebook.com/staflow`, `instagram.com/staflow`, `t.me/staflow`, `@staflow`, `/staflow` → your real pages
- The "Sample visual" illustrations → your real event photos

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Repo **Settings → Pages**
2. Source: **Deploy from a branch** → Branch: `main`, folder `/ (root)` → **Save**
3. After a minute or two the site is live at `https://choco731.github.io/staflow.landingpage.io/`
